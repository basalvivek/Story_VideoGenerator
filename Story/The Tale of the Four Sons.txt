The Tale of the Four Sons
Once upon a time in a quiet village, an old and wise father called his four sons. He handed each of them 100 gold coins and said:
“Go out into the world, use this money as you see fit, and return after three months. Show me what you have done with it.”

The sons accepted the challenge and went on their separate paths.

Son 1 – The Spender
The eldest son thought, “Life is short. Why not enjoy it?”
He went to the city, stayed in fancy inns, feasted every night, and bought beautiful clothes. When the three months were over, he returned home with nothing left—except some good memories and stories.
Son 2 – The Saver
The second son was cautious. He buried the money safely in a metal box under a tree.
He lived simply, spent nothing, and returned after three months with exactly the same 100 gold coins.
He said, “I didn’t risk anything. At least I didn’t lose what you gave me.”
Son 3 – The Investor
The third son found a small merchant who sold spices and offered to invest his money in the trade.
Together, they made profits selling in nearby towns. After three months, the son returned home with 200 gold coins and new knowledge about business.
Son 4 – The Builder
The youngest son went to a poor village where people lacked water.
He used the gold coins to help build a small well. The grateful villagers shared part of their harvest with him, and he also began selling the water to nearby areas.
He came back with 150 gold coins, many letters of gratitude, and the respect of an entire village.
